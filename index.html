3D Space Transformations

In Computer Graphics, transformations are mathematical operations that change an object's Position, Rotation, and Scale within a 3D coordinate system.

Rotation: Circular movement around an axis (X, Y, or Z). We use Euler Angles or Quaternions to calculate these orientations.

Scaling: Changing the size of the object. A scale of 1.0 is the original size.
